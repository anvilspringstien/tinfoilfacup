Welcome to BETA.

Oh dear. We’ve made Challenges.

Finding your nearest club and following your Tin Foil FA Cup was apparently not enough.

We decided you needed completely unnecessary reasons to become emotionally invested in this whole mad adventure.

So now there are Challenges.

Some are automated as your Campaign progresses. Others rely entirely on your honour, because we're not going to demand evidence that you ate a pie.

There may be dogs. There may be match-day coppers. There will definitely be pigeons — don’t ask.

Complete Challenges and fill your Trophy Cabinet with beer mats commemorating achievements that nobody outside Tin Foil FA Cup will understand or respect.

None of this is necessary.

Which is rather the point.

Still in BETA, we’ve left some of the machinery exposed so pull levers, press buttons — nothing important should explode.

More importantly, this BETA is now wired to Clubfinder for Campaign truth. So have fun.

Let’s Go!
