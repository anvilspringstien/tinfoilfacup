Keep the Magic

Keep your Cup Run close. Put Tin Foil FA Cup on your Home Screen, and make a private backup of your Campaign and Challenges progress.

Keep it on your Home Screen

iPhone or iPad — Safari: Open the Clubfinder website, tap Safari's Share button, choose Add to Home Screen, then Add (or enable Open as Web App if offered).

Android — Chrome: Open Clubfinder, tap the three-dot menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app, whichever your browser offers.

Computer: Bookmark the page using Cmd+D or Ctrl+D. Some browsers also offer an Install option.

A Home Screen shortcut is not a cloud account and is not an offline copy. Campaign data stays on the device and within the relevant browser/app storage.

Save your progress privately

Use Download Backup in the browser where your Campaign lives. Later, open this page in the new browser, installed app or domain and use Restore Backup.

Your backup can contain your postcode, pigeon name and Challenge progress. Keep the file private. It is created and restored on your device; nothing is uploaded to Tin Foil FA Cup. Restore replaces existing Campaign and Challenges progress in the current browser/app. This feature does not combine two histories.
