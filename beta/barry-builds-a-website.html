The Four Laws

The Four Laws of Tin Foil FA Cup will renew your love of The Cup, save your marriage and make you rich. It'll even make your kids like you!

  1. Make a Tin Foil FA Cup
  2. Find your nearest Qualifying Round
  3. Follow the Winner until you Can't Get Tickets
  4. Eat Pies

How did all this nonsense start?

It started with three pals, a bus and a bit of tin foil.

Tin Foil FA Cup was born in August 2023 out of a pact between three friends who shared a love of football and the FA Cup.

The plan was simple: start at the earliest qualifying rounds, then follow the winner to the next round. Keep going until Wembley — or until they couldn't get tickets.

So they made a Tin Foil FA Cup, got on the bus, went the footie and ate pies.

On the way home, the Four Laws mysteriously appeared as a nasty rash on the back of one of the trio.

Initially mistaken for shingles — he hadn't had his jab yet — the Laws were hurriedly copied down by Jan the following morning.

And that was that.

The luck of the draw took the Tin Foil FA Cup through Hebburn Town, Tadcaster Albion, Chester, Hartlepool, York City and Wigan before big guns Manchester United drew lowly Newport County.

Ten games in. 3,025 km. Players' lounges. Managers. Police escorts. New friends. An unreasonable number of pies.

But tickets for Newport County v Manchester United became rarer than rocking-horse poo, and the Third Law was merciless:

If you can't get tickets — that's it — YOU'RE OUT!

Four rounds short of Wembley, the first Tin Foil FA Cup Campaign was over.

But what a run. What a time. What an adventure. What pies.

Time to do it all again?

You bet.
